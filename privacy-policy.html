Beautyrocket Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blonde Nirvana LLC ("Blonde Nirvana LLC," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information through the Beautyrocket application ("Beautyrocket," "the App").

Beautyrocket is designed to help businesses manage Instagram content and messaging, including publishing content, managing comments, responding to direct messages, and related Instagram business activities.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how Beautyrocket is used and the permissions granted through Meta and Instagram, the App may access the following information:

  • Instagram account and profile information.
  • Instagram media, including posts, images, videos, and related information.
  • Comments and comment-related information.
  • Instagram direct messages and messaging-related information.
  • Information necessary to publish or manage Instagram content.
  • Instagram account insights and analytics when the applicable permission is granted.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use information accessed through Beautyrocket only as reasonably necessary to provide and operate the App's features.

This may include:

  • Publishing and managing Instagram content.
  • Managing and responding to Instagram comments.
  • Receiving and responding to Instagram direct messages.
  • Managing Instagram business communications.
  • Providing requested automation and messaging functionality.
  • Monitoring and improving the performance and reliability of the App.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing technical or security issues.

3. Meta and Instagram Platform Information

Beautyrocket uses services and APIs provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta") to connect with Instagram and related Meta services.

Information obtained through Meta's platforms is handled in accordance with applicable Meta platform requirements and permissions granted by the Instagram account owner.

Meta may independently collect and process information according to its own privacy policies, terms, and platform rules.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent Instagram information accessed through Beautyrocket.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate, host, secure, maintain, or improve Beautyrocket. These providers are permitted to process information only as necessary to provide their services to us.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, governmental requests, enforce our rights, protect the security of the App, or prevent fraud or abuse.

5. Third-Party Services

Beautyrocket may rely on third-party infrastructure and technology providers to operate the App.

These providers may include Meta Platforms, Inc. and hosting or technical infrastructure providers such as Render.

Third-party services may process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Beautyrocket's functionality, maintain the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate business interests.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Access Tokens and Account Authorization

Beautyrocket may use access tokens or other authorization credentials provided through Meta's authorization systems to perform actions that the Instagram account owner has authorized.

Users should not share their access tokens or other authorization credentials with unauthorized individuals.

Access to Instagram through Beautyrocket may be revoked by removing or disconnecting the applicable authorization through Meta or Instagram.

9. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with Beautyrocket by contacting us at the email address listed below.

Users may also disconnect or revoke Beautyrocket's access to their Instagram account through the applicable Meta or Instagram account settings.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to delete applicable information, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

Beautyrocket is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through Beautyrocket.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Beautyrocket, our practices, applicable law, or platform requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the effective date will be updated as appropriate.
